Student AI competencies

Source: Fengchun Miao, Kelly Shiohira and Natalie Lao, UNESCO, AI competency framework for students (2024), official publication page; full framework, DOI 10.54675/JKJB9835.

Access and design: Official overview and parts of framework PDF inspected. Normative curriculum framework, not a trial or a participant sample. Twelve competencies span human-centred mindset, ethics, AI techniques and applications, and system design, across understand, apply and create. It recommends agency and critical judgment. This specifies educational goals; it does not show that any particular curriculum causes improved learning.
