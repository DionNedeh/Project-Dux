Age-appropriate implementation

Children · 1 min read

Source: UNESCO, Guidance for generative AI in education and research, 7 September 2023, official overview.

Access and design: Official overview inspected; full guidance not fully reviewed. Policy and pedagogical guidance, not a trial. It calls for privacy protections, age limits for independent conversations, and age-appropriate validation and design. Specific age thresholds and current jurisdiction-specific legal requirements have not been verified here; avoid presenting any recommendation as a universal law.
