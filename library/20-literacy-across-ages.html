Building AI literacy across ages

AI literacy is not confined to a single application. A person may encounter AI in a search result, a recommendation, a school tool or a workplace process without having chosen a chatbot. The OECD and European Commission framework describes knowledge, skills and attitudes for primary and secondary learners, including critical evaluation and responsible use. It is a framework of goals, not an evaluation of a particular lesson. S18

Begin with a familiar decision

A younger learner might examine whether a generated description matches a picture and ask an adult for help. An older learner might compare source claims, discuss whose perspectives are missing, or examine a generated citation. An adult learner might evaluate a tool against professional responsibilities or compare explanations with a reliable reference. These are Dux examples, not proven age bands or validated interventions.

Practice judgment in both directions

Appropriate reliance involves noticing errors and recognizing when evidence supports a useful answer. The small Estonian study found that one short text did not significantly reduce reliance on incorrect screenshots, while participants in the intervention rejected more correct answers. The study does not show that all AI literacy instruction fails. S07

A university preprint randomized students to four forms of instruction about educational prompting and reports measured gains on a prompting rubric. Only 431 of 979 randomized course enrollees remained in its complete-case analysis, and there was no direct between-condition difference on the final exam. Prompt quality and general academic learning are different outcomes. S20

Questions for educators

What does the learner already know? What can be demonstrated without requiring an account? How will the activity handle privacy and accessibility? Can the learner use a non-AI route? What would count as a useful change in judgment after the lesson? Learners should have a meaningful opportunity to disagree or stop. S02 S05
