Relationships, community and belonging

Education happens between people as well as through materials. A tool may help a learner prepare questions or find an explanation, but educational quality also involves human connection, classroom participation and the ability to seek help. UNESCO’s broader technology report places learners’ interests and human interaction at the centre. S15

What one new study asks

An accepted 2026 paper reports a six-section randomized study in one Chinese university of a combined preparation protocol involving AI, reflection, question generation and instructor responsiveness. It reports a section-level belonging difference, but the exact randomization tests and small number of sections warrant care. The abstract has been inspected, not the full methods; the intervention does not isolate the AI component. S17

Design for connection

As a Dux proposal, invite learners to bring unresolved questions to peers or a teacher, reserve time for discussion, and give people a way to ask a human for help. Check whether the activity increases or reduces participation for different learners. Provide alternatives for people who need different forms of communication.

Children's relationships

UNICEF has identified distinct child-rights concerns around conversational and companion-like systems. A tool should not be presented as a substitute for trusted people; family and institutional safeguards should be adapted to the child and setting. The guidance is not a measurement of prevalence.
