OECD and European Commission AI literacy framework

AI literacy · 1 min read

Source: OECD and European Commission, Empowering Learners for the Age of AI: An AI Literacy Framework for Primary and Secondary Education, 18 June 2026, doi:10.1787/65cd27d4-en.

Access and design: Official overview and abstract inspected; complete 64-page framework not fully reviewed. Normative framework for desired learning outcomes; no participant count or intervention effect. It includes understanding, critical evaluation and ethical and creative use. It complements, rather than experimentally validates, UNESCO’s framework. Specific activities and age progression require review of the full report.
