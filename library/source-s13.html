AI risk management framework

Source: Elham Tabassi, National Institute of Standards and Technology, Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0), 26 January 2023, doi:10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1; core functions.

Access and design: NIST publication overview and online core inspected. Voluntary, non-sector-specific governance framework, not a school efficacy study or a binding universal standard. It organizes activities around govern, map, measure and manage, and says the functions are not a checklist. NIST notes a revision is in progress; check current status before use.
