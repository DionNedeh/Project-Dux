Conversational AI and children

Source: UNICEF, When AI becomes a friend: Child rights risks, harms, and regulatory responses to AI chatbots and companions, June 2026, official document page.

Access and design: Issuer overview inspected, not full policy brief. Policy analysis compares approaches across six jurisdictions as of May 2026; six is a jurisdiction count, not children or experiments. It identifies risks and recommends preventive safeguards and accountability. The overview does not quantify risk or establish prevalence.
