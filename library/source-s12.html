Child privacy and schools

Source: U.S. Federal Trade Commission, Complying with COPPA: Frequently Asked Questions, section N, webpage accessed 12 November 2026.

Access and design: Selected agency FAQ on schools inspected; not a trial. It describes limited circumstances in which a school may act for a parent in relation to educational services, and distinguishes school-benefit uses from other commercial purposes. The detailed requirements and applicable law depend on context and may change. Dux does not provide legal advice or assert that this US guidance applies elsewhere.
