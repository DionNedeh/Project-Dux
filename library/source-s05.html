Children's rights and safeguards

Children · 1 min read

Source: UNICEF Innocenti, Guidance on AI and children, version 3.0, December 2025, official report page.

Access and design: Issuer's overview inspected; complete PDF not reviewed. Rights-based guidance informed by consultation and existing evidence, including a twelve-country study; that number is countries, not a participant count. The overview lists safety, privacy, fairness, transparency, accountability, development, inclusion and children's AI skills among ten requirements. These are recommendations, not evidence that a particular safeguard has been validated or legal advice.
