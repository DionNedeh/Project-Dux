Education technology in context

Access and inclusion · 1 min read

Source: UNESCO Global Education Monitoring Report Team, Technology in education: A tool on whose terms?, 2023, official publication page.

Access and design: Issuer’s overview and selected summary text inspected, not every underlying study. The report considers education technology through relevance, equity, scalability and sustainability. Its broader education-technology scope must not be recast as a trial of generative AI. It advocates learning centred on people and attention to potential adverse consequences.
