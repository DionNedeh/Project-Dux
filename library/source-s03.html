Educator competencies

Teaching · 1 min read

Source: UNESCO, AI competency framework for teachers (2024), official framework PDF.

Access and design: Selected PDF sections inspected; normative professional-development framework, not a controlled outcome study. It emphasizes teacher agency, privacy, critical review, human interaction and learner control. Its recommendation to test pedagogical strategies is a program-design principle, not proof of efficacy.
